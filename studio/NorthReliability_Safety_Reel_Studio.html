THE 4-STEP ZERO-ENERGY PROTOCOL

NO PM OR BREAKDOWN SKIPS THESE 4 STEPS

01
ISOLATE & TAG CSA Z460

02
BLEED, VENT & BLOCK 0 PSI / PINNED

A padlock on the breaker does not mean the machine is dead. Most serious maintenance injuries happen after the switch is off—because trapped hydraulic pressure, raised loads, or hot chemical lines were never bled or blocked.

SLIDE 2 · [0.25s lead-in → speak → 0.35s hold] …

Under Canadian standard C S A Z-four-sixty and W H M I S, three rules protect your team: one lock per person with a try-start test, checking Safety Data Sheet Section Eight before breaking lines, and wearing rated P P E.

SLIDE 3 · [0.25s lead-in → speak → 0.35s hold] …

True reliability means every technician goes home whole. Before turning a wrench: isolate and hang your personal lock, bleed and pin all stored energy to zero, verify zero voltage with a try-start test, and match your P P E.
